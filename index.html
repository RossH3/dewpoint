Dew point comfort scale

    Forecast from the National Weather Service hourly forecast (api.weather.gov). No account or key needed.

    Humidity climbs at night as the air cools, even when the moisture in the air stays the same. The dew point is the steadier measure of how sticky it feels.

    Pressure: low means air rising and spreading out, bringing clouds and rain. High means air sinking and packing down, keeping it clear and dry. Average is 29.92 in; most weather falls between 29.5 and 30.5.

    Chart lines are drawn as smooth curves through the hourly values. Temperature gets a light 3-hour average, the dew point a slightly wider (5-hour, weighted) one, and pressure a 12-hour average that removes its daily tide. Labels, the readout and the hourly list show the actual numbers.
