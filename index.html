    Forecast from the National Weather Service hourly forecast (api.weather.gov). No account or key needed.

    Pressure: low means air rising and spreading out, bringing clouds and rain. High means air sinking and packing down, keeping it clear and dry. Average is 29.92 in; most weather falls between 29.5 and 30.5.

    Chart lines are drawn as smooth curves through the hourly values. The dew point also gets a light 3-hour average, and pressure a 12-hour average that removes its daily tide. Labels and the hourly list show the actual numbers.
